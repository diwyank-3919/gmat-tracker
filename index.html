A focused toolkit that quietly does the bookkeeping — so your attention stays on studying, not spreadsheets.

Start, pause and resume a live timer that auto-saves to the cloud the moment you stop. Studying past midnight? Log it to the previous day in one tap, so those late hours count where they belong.
Four signature rings read your prep in a second: Readiness, Weekly Pace, Focus and a Burnout meter that tells you when to push and when to rest.
A heuristic engine turns your mock scores and study consistency into a predicted score range — with a confidence band that tightens as you log more data, and a clear read on your biggest lever.
Click any day to open a 24-hour timeline of when you actually studied. Score Console learns your best slot, your most consistent hour, and your longest unbroken focus session.
